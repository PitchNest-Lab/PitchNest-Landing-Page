Platform FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes PitchNest a fundraising system rather than a practice tool?

Practice tools simply let you record your voice. PitchNest is a comprehensive simulation system that builds a multi-persona AI investor panel. They actually read your deck, debate with each other in real-time, score you on 5 critical business dimensions, and produce a PDF red-flag action list.

How realistic is the AI Investment Committee?

Our committee simulates 4 distinct investor archetypes: an Aggressive VC focused strictly on margins, a product-oriented Angel Investor, a deeply technical VC looking for wrapper tools, and a Skeptical Partner checking monetization risk. They challenge your arguments and vote collectively.

Is the Health Check Analyzer deck-aware?

Yes. When you upload your deck PDF or input your company metrics, the AI cross-references it with sector standards in AI devtools, climate tech, hardware, or biotech, instantly checking your revenue assumptions and GTM loops for core vulnerabilities.

Is my startup data secure and private?

Absolutely. We encrypt all deck uploads and voice sessions. Your startup profile only lists on our public Discovery Catalog if you explicitly opt-in to show verified score milestones to matched investor partners.
